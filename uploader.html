Hours of the Past uploader

The Hours of the Past uploader is a private, single-user tool run by Quiet Engine Media. It uploads the owner's own finished videos and their thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel, Hours of the Past, as private or unlisted videos. The owner publishes them himself.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What it accesses. Only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos and set their thumbnails on the owner's channel. It may try to look up the status of a video it has just uploaded. It does not change or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

What it does with the data. It sends the owner's own video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to YouTube, as private or unlisted. The owner's AI assistant (which runs on Anthropic's Claude) prepares and runs each upload. On the owner's computer it keeps a log of each upload: time, file name, a fingerprint of each file, the settings sent, YouTube's response and the video ID. No viewer or third-party data is collected.

Sharing. No data is sold or used for advertising. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the purpose above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security. Sign-in credentials are kept in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer. The upload log is kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's encrypted backups.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Security page, under third-party access; the tool can also revoke its own access and delete its credentials.
