Hours of the Past uploader

The Hours of the Past uploader is a private, single-user tool run by Quiet Engine Media. It uploads the owner's own finished videos and their thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel, Hours of the Past, as private or unlisted videos. The owner publishes them himself.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What it accesses. Only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos and set their thumbnails on the owner's channel. It does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

What it does with the data. It sends the owner's own video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to YouTube. On the owner's computer it keeps a log of each upload (time, file name, the video ID YouTube returns). No viewer or third-party data is collected.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the purpose above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security. Sign-in credentials are stored in the operating system's credential store on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any outside server.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Security page, under third-party access.
