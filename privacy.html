Martin Intake: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who uses it

Martin Intake is a private tool used only by its owner, Quiet Engine Media, for the business's own email account. It is not available to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

Reading (the gmail.readonly permission): it reads new messages arriving in the business inbox from any sender, and the headers of messages in Spam. It cannot change or delete email with this permission.

Drafting and sending (the gmail.compose permission, a separate sign-in): it creates drafts for the owner to review, and sends plain-text messages written by the owner's own software to a short list of contacts the owner has approved. It never sends text copied from received email, and it does not delete mail.

What it does with the data

Messages from the owner are filed as notes in the owner's private records. Other mail is sorted by rules into categories such as security alerts, receipts, business enquiries and routine notices, and the owner is notified on their phone of important items (sender and subject only). For sorting and for the owner's review it keeps, on the owner's computer: sender, subject, date and a short plain-text excerpt (up to 2,000 characters); a receipts list for business records; sender domains for 14 days; Spam headers for up to 45 days; and activity logs with message IDs and sorting results (logs of sent mail record recipient, subject and a fingerprint of the text, not the text itself).

Who else receives it

Some email text (sender, subject and up to 2,000 characters of plain text) is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to classify and summarise it; that step has no tools and cannot take actions. The owner's AI assistant, which also runs on Claude, reads the resulting notes and summaries. Anthropic handles this text under its own terms for the owner's account, and the owner has turned off Anthropic's use of their content for model training. No other outside service receives it. No data is sold or used for advertising. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the purposes above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and this tool does not use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Sign-in credentials are kept in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer. Notes and working data are kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's encrypted backups, including an encrypted copy in the owner's own cloud storage. They are not published or shared.

Keeping and deleting

Notes are kept as long as the owner's records need them; the owner can delete them at any time. Anyone whose message was processed can ask for it to be deleted by writing to the contact address below.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Security page, under third-party access, which stops all reading and sending.
