Martin Intake

Martin Intake is a private, single-user tool run by Quiet Engine Media. It reads the business inbox, sorts incoming mail for the owner, files the owner's own messages as notes in the owner's records, prepares drafts, and sends short plain-text messages to a few contacts the owner has approved. The owner's AI assistant helps sort and summarise the mail.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.
