Martin Personal Tools: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who runs it and who uses it

Martin Personal Tools is a private tool operated by Quiet Engine Media for the personal use of its owner and one other person the owner has invited, each signing in with their own Google Account. It is not available to the public. The owner runs the tool on the owner's computer, so the owner (and the owner's AI assistant, below) can see the data the tool collects for both people, including the invited person's.

What it accesses

Gmail (the gmail.modify permission): it reads the signed-in person's messages; adds or removes its own labels (all beginning "Martin/"); labels promotional messages (they stay in the inbox: the tool does not archive mail); saves PDF, CSV, OFX or QFX statement attachments from senders on a list the owner has approved; and creates drafts, which stay unsent until the person sends them. The Gmail permission Google offers is broader than this. The tool is built not to send email, delete or trash messages, mark messages read, forward mail, or change any Gmail setting or filter; a software check refuses those requests before they reach Google.

Google Calendar (the calendar.events permission): it reads, adds, updates and removes entries on a few dedicated calendars the owner created for this purpose, and only entries the tool itself created there.

What it keeps

On the owner's computer, kept separately for each person: message ids, sender, subject, date and the label applied; for messages filed as expected replies, promotions or statements, a cleaned copy of the message text (links reduced to their website name, long numbers and codes masked, at most 8,000 characters); a list of offers and coupon codes; for receipts, refunds, deposit notices and statement-ready notices, and for other messages that show a payment amount, the sender, date, amount (and the card's last four digits or a tip, when shown) and the few lines they were read from, which the owner uses to keep the household budget and to match bank or card lines (also kept on the owner's own computers); suggested school calendar entries read from school emails; saved statement files; calendar entries the tool manages; and activity logs. Any single message can also be shown to the owner's assistant on request. Links in messages are never opened. The owner's computer is backed up in encrypted form, including an encrypted copy in the owner's own cloud storage. Drafts and labels the tool creates live in the person's own Gmail.

Who else receives it

The owner's personal AI assistant runs on Anthropic's Claude service, so message content the tool reads is sent to Anthropic to be sorted and summarised for the person; Anthropic handles it under its own terms for the owner's account, and the owner has turned off Anthropic's use of their content for model training. No other outside service receives it. No data is sold or used for advertising. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the purposes above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and this tool does not use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Data is kept while the person uses the tool. Either person can ask for their data to be deleted, from the owner's computer and its backups, by writing to sources@quietenginemedia.com; it is deleted within 30 days.

Storage and security

Sign-in credentials and saved statement files are kept in folders that only the owner's computer account can read. Everything else is kept on the owner's password-protected computer and is never published.

Revoking access

Either person can remove access at any time from their Google Account's Security page, under third-party access, which stops all further reading.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sources@quietenginemedia.com
